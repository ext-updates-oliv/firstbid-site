How it works

No AI negotiation, no black box — just the fastest possible first contact.

01

New order detected instantly

FirstBid watches your Eldorado.gg seller account 24/7 and catches new boosting requests the moment they're posted.

02

Reads the exact request

Mode, group size (solo/duo), current rank, and desired rank — matched against the pricing table you set up yourself.

03

Sends the offer, in seconds

Price, delivery time, and a personalized opening message go out automatically — before the buyer moves on to the next seller.

04

You take it from there

FirstBid doesn't negotiate for you. Once the first message is out, the conversation is 100% yours — no AI pretending to be you. It just pings your Telegram when the buyer replies.
