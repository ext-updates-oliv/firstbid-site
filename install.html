Sign in with your purchase email and the 6-digit code sent to it. No password is needed.

First Eldorado connection

  1. On a computer, open FirstBid Web in Google Chrome.
  2. Install FirstBid Connector.
  3. Sign in to Eldorado in that same Chrome profile.
  4. Return to FirstBid Web and click Connect.

After this first connection, configure and monitor FirstBid from a desktop or phone browser. Hosting is included, so you do not need to keep your PC on or rent a personal VPS.

Moving from desktop 3.1.4?

Open the desktop app once and click Move my bot to FirstBid Web. Your settings move to the hosted version. Desktop 3.1.4 is frozen and receives no new features.

Notifications wherever you are

Turn on browser notifications from the Notifications section. FirstBid Web can link your phone there too, so buyer replies and important bot events reach both desktop and mobile.
