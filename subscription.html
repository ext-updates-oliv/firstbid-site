Short answers

Subscription FAQ

Where do I cancel?

Sign in above, choose the subscription and confirm cancellation. Access remains active through the paid period.

Where do I resume or change my card?

Those two actions use the SellAuth payment portal because its admin API does not provide them to FirstBid.

What happens when I pay with crypto?

Crypto is not charged automatically. SellAuth sends a renewal invoice by email; pay that invoice to continue.

What about an older one-time license?

It keeps its existing access period and appears with your account. The next plan purchased for that game is a subscription.
