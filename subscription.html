Subscription control

Keep the bot active. Keep control.

Every FirstBid plan is a subscription for the billing period you choose. Card and PayPal renew automatically. Crypto and other payment methods receive a renewal invoice by email. You can cancel anytime and keep access until the paid period ends.

Manage my subscription See plans

  1. 01 · Choose

    3, 7 or 30 days

    Choose one game or the all-games pass. The same billing period repeats until you cancel.

  2. 02 · Renew

    Automatic or by invoice

    Stripe and PayPal Checkout charge automatically. Other methods, including crypto, send a renewal invoice to your purchase email.

  3. 03 · Control

    Cancel when you want

    Cancellation is scheduled for the end of the paid period. You keep the time you already paid for.

Short answers

Subscription FAQ

Where do I cancel or resume?

Use the SellAuth customer panel. It lets you cancel, resume, and see your subscriptions in one place.

Where do I change my card?

Open the same customer panel and use the payment-management option for your subscription.

What happens when I pay with crypto?

Crypto is not charged automatically. SellAuth sends a renewal invoice by email before the next period; pay that invoice to continue.

Do I lose access as soon as I cancel?

No. Cancellation happens at the end of the period you already paid for.

What about an older one-time license?

It keeps its existing access period. The next plan purchased for that game is a subscription.
